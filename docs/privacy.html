Privacy Policy — FlatShift

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

FlatShift ("we", "our", or "the App") is built with a strict offline-first, zero-ad, zero-tracking architecture. Your work schedules, job hourly rates, shift clock-ins, overtime breakdowns, and exported timesheets stay exclusively on your device.

1. Zero Cloud Data Collection

FlatShift does not require an account, login, or cloud sync. We never transmit, collect, sell, or store your jobs, pay rates, work schedules, notes, or generated PDF/CSV timesheets on any developer servers.

2. Notifications (Optional Trial Reminder)

If you choose the Annual Pro plan with a 3-day free trial, FlatShift requests local notification permission solely to schedule a single on-device reminder 24 hours before your free trial ends.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All purchases and subscriptions are processed directly by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit 2). We never access or store your payment credentials.

4. Zero Advertising & Zero Tracking

FlatShift contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero tracking identifiers, and zero behavioral analytics.

5. Data Export & Deletion

You can export all your jobs and shifts as a local JSON backup or CSV spreadsheet anytime from Jobs & Settings, or permanently delete all local data by uninstalling the app.
