Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn on Night-Shift Dark Mode?
Tap the Moon/Sun icon in the top bar of Clock & Pay or Calendar, or select Night Shift under Jobs & Settings → Theme Mode.

How do I configure weekly-only overtime (e.g., 3×12 nurse shifts with OT only after 40h/wk)?
Go to Jobs & Settings → tap your Job → set Daily OT After to 0 and Weekly OT After to 40.

How do I restore my Lifetime Pro purchase on a new iPhone?
Open FlatShift → Jobs & Settings → tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the same Apple Account.

How do I back up my shifts before switching devices?
Go to Jobs & Settings → tap "Export Full Local Backup (JSON)" and save to Files or iCloud Drive, then tap "Restore from JSON Backup" on your new device.
